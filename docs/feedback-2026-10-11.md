Status: **open**, **done** (with commit), **hardware** (waiting on parts).

| # | Area | Feedback | Status |
|---|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Radio cable, 10.0.1.183 | The AIOC drops off USB right after the node transmits: twice out of two (17:50:43, 8 s after a transmit; and after "bench test 11" at 18:30:06). The kernel logs `device descriptor read/64, error -71` and a failed port power cycle. Replugging doesn't help: the Pi Zero's USB controller stays stuck until a reboot. During the second drop the radio stayed keyed for 6 s or more with no packet audio (10.0.0.65 heard a carrier at about −37 dBFS and decoded nothing). 10.0.0.65's AIOC transmitted 7 times without a drop. Third time at 18:55:03, monitored: the drop follows 10.0.1.183's own transmit (an ack), and its **receive works** (it decoded 10.0.0.65's message just before). So the cable and radio work until 10.0.1.183 keys up. Likely a bad cable (new cables are on the way); RF into the USB or a power sag on transmit are the other candidates. Swapping the AIOCs between the nodes would tell cable from Pi/radio. | hardware |
| 2 | Radio check | Check radio passed 10.0.1.183 with its cable gone: graywolf's API kept listing the device and its last level (−47 dBFS), and its channel stayed "live". Fixed: the app reads the node's sound cards (`/proc/asound/cards`) and fails **Audio devices** when the channel's card is missing, marks the level reading stale, and shows **Radio cable missing** on the panel and the Admin graywolf card. A WARN is logged once when it goes missing. Only when graywolf runs on the same machine. Verified on 10.0.1.183 with the cable gone, and passing after the reboot. | done |
| 3 | Radio check, HQ without a radio | "Modem keeping up" showed ok on 10.0.1.88, which has no radio channel (graywolf's modem runs anyway). Now unchecked, like the other radio items, until a channel exists. | done |
| 4 | graywolf channel settings, 10.0.0.65 | Its channel has empty demodulator settings (profile, slicers, fix_bits, decoders: blank or 0), while 10.0.1.183 has profile A, 1 slicer, fix_bits none, 1 decoder. It may explain some of 10.0.0.65's damaged frames (9 bad, 0 good since graywolf started), but nothing was confirmed while 10.0.1.183's cable kept dropping. **Set to match on 2026-10-11** (user approved) through graywolf's API (`PUT /api/channels/1`, only the channel's own fields); the modem restarted with the 2048-frame buffer and 24 kHz audio kept. Effect on decoding to be seen on air. | done |
| 5 | graywolf logins | graywolf's admin user isn't the same on every node. checkin-board uses its own graywolf login, so the app isn't affected; operators need to know which login each node's graywolf UI uses. | note |
| 6 | RF bench (phase 12 g) | Blocked by item 1. The scripted bench race tool is ready (`benchrace`, [`docs/bench-race.md`](bench-race.md)) and passes against simulated nodes; run it on air once the cables work. | hardware |
| 7 | Node logs | Feedback 2026-10-09 item 9: done. The journal stays in RAM (Raspberry Pi OS already sets `Storage=volatile`; no syslog daemon), now 28 MB on the Pi Zeros and 60 MB on the Pi 3, with graywolf capped at 500 lines per 30 s. Installed on all three nodes. | done |
